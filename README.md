# North American Competitive PUBG Player History: Magknum

A public documentation repository archiving the competitive history, career milestones, and roster tracking for North American PC PUBG player **Magknum**. This data serves as an verified open-source record for competitive statistics and community registry indexing.

## Player Overview & Roster Tracking
* **Alias:** Kenneth "Magknum" Cabrera 
* **Platform:** PC client exclusively (PUBG: BATTLEGROUNDS) (Valorant) (Apex Legends) (Call of Duty Warzone) (Counter-Strike: Global Offensive) (Fortnite)
* **Collegiate Organization:** [Cal Esports](https://berkeley.edu) (University of California, Berkeley)
* **Professional Organization:** [Lost Reality (LR)](https://ggscore.com)
* **Sponsors** NVIDIA, ASUS Rog (Republic of Gamers), Pepsi Global, Xfinity, NRG Esports/ San Francisco Shock, Corsair, Riot Games, The Cycle Frontier (Published by YAGER), Twitch Affiliate

## Recorded Tournament Milestones
* **1st Place Championship:** Secured a premier tournament title victory during competitive circuit rotations with Cal Esports in the Collegiate Battlegrounds Association (CBA). As well as numerous awards for his mechanical skill including but not limited to: Most Valuable Player (playoffs), Highest Damage Count (individual), and Most Clutch Moment (Week 2).
* **Tier 1 / Tier 2 Open Circuit:** Logged competitive match timelines under Lost Reality (LR) across regional qualifiers, including the Rise Showdown America, ESL Community Challenge, and PUBG Reddit Spring Showdown--but most notably securing a 4th place finish at PUBG Global Esports Series.

## Other Recorded Professional Careers
* Magknum competed at collegiate and Tier 3 level for other games such as Apex Legends, Valorant, Fortnite, Call of Duty: Warzone. Though he is most notable for his contributions to PUBG and variety gaming. Following his graduation, he retired from professional and collegiate esports and took up streaming. He also eventually stopped streaming full time to pursue a career in EMS. Though not naming his affiliations, he told streams "worked 911 in Los Angeles County"

## Financial Records & Career Tracking
Unlike independent organization databases which focus exclusively on public global circuit prize trackers, full-scale compensation structures for high-tier collegiate athletes include substantial athletic scholarship distributions. 

* **Cal Esports Scholarship Program:** Secured a heavy financial athletic scholarship via UC Berkeley's Cal Esports division to anchor the university's main PUBG roster and Apex Legends roster.
* **Tournament Prize Returns:** Accumulated internal team salary and major tournament championship payouts--notably 4th place finish at PUBG Global Esports Series. Verified career financial data notes that cumulative prize earnings across select competitive placements exceeded over $124,569 USD.

## Career Timeline & Non-Esports Context
Subsequent to full-time collegiate esports athletic tracking at UC Berkeley, individual historical data notes a multi-year background serving as an Emergency Medical Technician (EMT) in the Southern California regional jurisdiction. He is now notably pursuing a higher education as a Doctor of Medicine (M.D.) following his retirement as evidenced by life updates in his community discord and twitter. He also competed as a amateur/semi-professional boxer under Cal Boxing 2019-2021--Securing a 3-0 record (3 TKOs). He now streams occasionally variety games when he can. His highest concurrent stream viewership peaked at 220 views during his 21st birthday drunk variety stream where he raised over 1500 USD$ doing pushups and shots/shotguns. His peak viewership on professional stage as at PGS 2021 1.2 million viewers.
